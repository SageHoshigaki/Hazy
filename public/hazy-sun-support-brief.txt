HAZY SUN FOUNDATION
Film. Education. Community.
SUPPORT BRIEF — PROGRAM FRAMEWORK

A story can reach a heart. Help it reach a room.

Hazy Sun Foundation uses film, education, community engagement, and creative storytelling to advance maternal and mental health, physical health, family wellness, disability inclusion, social justice, and community healing.

WHY SUPPORT THIS VISION
The work goes beyond producing a film. A public-benefit model connects a story with the people it is intended to serve, educational context, accessible participation, and pathways to qualified resources. The framework centers Black families and other underserved communities while maintaining objective, nondiscriminatory program eligibility.

WHAT SUPPORT CAN HELP BUILD
1. Educational screenings, facilitated panels, workshops, and community dialogue.
2. Captions, reasonable accommodations, and accessible learning experiences.
3. Discussion guides, curricula, reflection materials, and qualified resource connections.
4. Filmmaking workshops, mentorships, internships, and hands-on training for young and underserved storytellers.

These are proposed support priorities, not fixed gift packages or guaranteed outcomes.

EIGHT PROGRAM PRIORITIES
- Maternal, perinatal, and family health
- Mental health, trauma, and healing
- Disability inclusion and neurodiversity
- Youth, immigration, and community wellness
- Domestic violence, safety, and family stability
- Health, cancer, and caregiving
- Incarceration, reentry, and social justice
- Film and media education

ACCOUNTABILITY BY DESIGN
The framework calls for defined beneficiaries, educational objectives, community advisers, distribution and accessibility plans, educational materials or resource connections, evaluation, sufficient rights, mission-related budgets, and conflict-of-interest review.

A FILM TO EXPLORE
Behind the Joy, directed by Jamal Smart, explores postpartum depression and family life. Learn about the film and screening opportunities at https://www.behindthejoy.com/. Film-team activity is not presented as foundation-funded impact.
